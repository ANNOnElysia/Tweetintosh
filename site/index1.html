Five languages, switched live. Settings changes the whole interface with no restart. A Mincho typeface is there for people who would rather read in a serif.

What is in it. Almost everything you would open X for, and a few things that only make sense on a Mac.

Build it yourself. You need macOS 15 or later and the Swift command line tools. Xcode is not required. The finished app is about 5 MB.
